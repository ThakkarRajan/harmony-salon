## 🔧 **What You Need to Do (One-Time Setup)**

### **Your To-Do List:**

**□ Step 1:** Go to https://www.emailjs.com  
**□ Step 2:** Sign up (free)  
**□ Step 3:** Connect your Gmail (shahshreya933@gmail.com)  
**□ Step 4:** Create email template (copy from guide)  
**□ Step 5:** Get 3 IDs:
   - Service ID
   - Template ID
   - Public Key

**□ Step 6:** Add to `.env.local` file:
```env
NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_SERVICE_ID=service_xxxxx
NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_TEMPLATE_ID=template_xxxxx
NEXT_PUBLIC_EMAILJS_PUBLIC_KEY=xxxxxxxxxxxxx
```

**□ Step 7:** Test the form!

**Time needed:** 10-15 minutes total
